# Abstract

> 来源: VisionZip: Longer is Better but Not Necessary in Vision Language Models (CVPR 2025)

---

## 📄 原文

> 💡 **Section 概览**: Abstract 回答三个问题——视觉 token 有什么问题？VisionZip 怎么解决？效果如何？

Recent advancements in vision-language models have enhanced performance by increasing the length of visual tokens, making them much longer than text tokens and significantly raising computational costs. However, we observe that the visual tokens generated by popular vision encoders, such as CLIP and SigLIP, contain significant redundancy.

> 💡 **批注**: 核心观察——CLIP/SigLIP 产生的视觉 token 大量冗余。这是整篇论文的出发点。不是 LLM 端发现的冗余，而是 **vision encoder 自己就已经冗余了**。

To address this, we introduce VisionZip, a simple yet effective method that selects a set of informative tokens for input to the language model, reducing visual token redundancy and improving efficiency while maintaining model performance. The proposed VisionZip can be widely applied to image and video understanding tasks and is well-suited for multi-turn dialogues in real-world scenarios, where previous methods tend to underperform.

> 💡 **批注**: VisionZip 的三个关键词：
> 1. **Text-agnostic** — 不依赖问题文本来选 token，在 vision encoder 端就做好了
> 2. **Training-free** — 可以直接用，也可以选择微调 projector
> 3. **Multi-turn friendly** — 因为不依赖文本，所以多轮对话不会掉性能（这是对 FastV/SparseVLM 的致命一击）

Experimental results show that VisionZip outperforms the previous state-of-the-art method by at least 5% performance gains across nearly all settings. Moreover, our method significantly enhances model inference speed, improving the prefilling time by 8× and enabling the LLaVA-Next 13B model to infer faster than the LLaVA-Next 7B model while achieving better results.

> 💡 **批注**: 关键数字速查：
> - 比 SOTA 高 **≥5%**
> - Prefilling 加速 **8×**
> - 13B + VisionZip **比 7B 原版更快更好**（这个结论很有说服力）

Furthermore, we analyze the causes of this redundancy and encourage the community to focus on extracting better visual features rather than merely increasing token length.

> 💡 **批注**: 最后一句是 call to action——别再堆 token 数了，该想想怎么让 vision encoder 输出更高质量的特征。

## 💡 Section 总结

### 核心洞察
1. 视觉 token 存在严重冗余（来自 vision encoder 本身）
2. VisionZip 在 vision encoder 端做 text-agnostic 压缩
3. 两步策略：dominant token selection + similarity-based merging
4. 适用于图像、视频、多轮对话

### 关键数字
| 指标 | 数值 |
|------|------|
| 性能增益 vs SOTA | ≥5% |
| Prefilling 加速 | 8× |
| 13B vs 7B | 更快且更好 |
