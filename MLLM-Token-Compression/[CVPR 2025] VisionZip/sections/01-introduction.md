### 1.1 VLM 的 Token 长度问题

Recently, the advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) has led to significant progress in Vision Language Models (VLMs). To integrate visual signals with textual semantics, existing VLMs typically utilize sequential visual representation, where images are converted into vision tokens and processed by an LLM decoder.

However, the promising performance of VLMs largely relies on the large amount of visual tokens. For example, in LLaVA-1.5, the number of visual tokens is 576, and in LLaVA-NeXT, a 672×672 image yields more than 576×5 = 2880 tokens, while the text tokens number only in the dozens to just over a hundred.

> 💡 **批注**: 数字对比很直观：
> ```
> LLaVA-1.5:    视觉 576 tokens vs 文本 ~几十到一百
> LLaVA-NeXT:   视觉 2880 tokens vs 文本 ~几十到一百
> 比例:          视觉 token 是文本的 20-50 倍！
> ```
> 这个不对称性是所有 efficient VLM 工作的动机来源。

These excessively long visual tokens consume a significant amount of memory and computation in the entire VLM, limiting the model's development in practical application scenarios such as edge computing, autonomous driving, and robotics. Furthermore, based on many previous studies, we know that the information contained in images is much sparser than in text. Hence, a natural question arises: **"Are all visual tokens necessary?"**

> 💡 **批注**: 这是论文的核心问题。答案显然是 "不是"，但关键在于 **怎么判断哪些是必要的**。

To explore this, we conduct a pilot study on the visual tokens generated by the widely used vision encoders, CLIP and SigLIP. As shown in Fig. 2, statistical and visual analysis reveal that only a few tokens receive high attention and contain a large amount of information, while most visual tokens receive minimal attention and aggregate limited information.

*Figure 2: 冗余可视化。attention 集中在少数 token 上，大部分 token 的 attention score 接近零。*

> 💡 **Figure 2 批读**:
> ```
> 左侧: CLIP/SigLIP 的 attention heatmap
> ├── 高亮区域 = 少数 dominant tokens (聚集了大量信息)
> └── 暗色区域 = 大量冗余 tokens (attention ≈ 0)
>
> 右侧: attention 分布直方图

> **大白话**: 想象一个 576 人的班级，老师（CLS token）只关注 5-10 个学霸，其余 560+ 个学生基本被忽略。VisionZip 就是只保留学霸 + 把其余学生的信息合并一下。

### 1.3 解决方案概述

Based on this observation, we develop a text-agnostic method named VisionZip to extract more informative visual tokens for the LLM. Our method can be used in training-free, fine-tuning, or training from scratch.

> 💡 **批注**: VisionZip 的三种使用模式：
> | 模式 | 描述 | 额外成本 |
> |------|------|----------|
> | Training-free | 直接推理时用 | 零 |
> | Fine-tuning (VisionZip‡) | 微调 projector 30min | 极小 |
> | Training from scratch | 训练阶段就用 | 省训练时间 |

Specifically, in training-free mode, we first select the **dominant tokens**, which receive significant attention and aggregate most of the image information. Then, to avoid missing small but potentially important details, we employ a **token merging strategy**, merging retained tokens based on their similarity to further extract informative contextual tokens.

> 💡 **批注**: 两步压缩策略（核心方法）：
> ```
> Step 1: Dominant Token Selection
> ├── 用 [CLS] token 的 attention 选出最重要的 token
> └── 这些 token 聚集了大部分图像信息
>
> Step 2: Contextual Token Merging
> ├── 剩余 token 按 Key 相似度合并
> └── 避免丢失小但重要的细节
>
> 最终输出: dominant tokens + contextual tokens → 送入 LLM
> ```

In fine-tuning mode, after selecting tokens to replace all raw visual tokens, the input token count decreases significantly, leading to a slight misalignment. To enhance results, we fine-tune the projector layer for 30 minutes with minimal data.

> 💡 **批注**: Fine-tuning 的成本极低——8×A800 只要 30 分钟，用 1/10 的 LLaVA-1.5 数据。甚至 3090 也能跑。这对实际部署非常友好。

## 💡 Section 总结

### 核心洞察
1. VLM 中视觉 token 数远超文本 token（20-50 倍），但信息密度远低于文本
2. Vision encoder（CLIP/SigLIP）的 attention 高度集中在少数 token，大部分 token 冗余
3. VisionZip 采用 text-agnostic 策略，在 vision encoder 端压缩，不依赖 LLM
4. 两步法：dominant selection + similarity merging

### 与 text-aware 方法的关键区别
| 特征 | VisionZip (text-agnostic) | FastV/SparseVLM (text-aware) |
|------|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| 压缩位置 | Vision encoder 端 | LLM 内部各层 |
| 是否依赖文本 | ❌ | ✅ |
| 多轮对话 | ✅ 天然支持 | ❌ KV cache 与旧问题绑定 |
| 效率 | 更高（LLM 浅层也省了） | 较低（浅层仍需全量计算） |
